# Part 8 — Creative Multiplication System

**One good shoot should feed a month of testing.** This system turns one piece of core footage into many ads, but only into variations that **test a real question**.

## The core idea: body constant, entry points vary

An ad has two parts:
- **The body** (roughly seconds 3 to the end): the argument, the mechanism, the proof, the taste, the CTA. Expensive to make and slow to change.
- **The entry point** (seconds 0–3, plus caption and CTA): the hook, first frame, on-screen text and length. Cheap to change, and it has the **biggest effect on who watches and how many**.

So we **lock the body** and **multiply the entry points**.

---

## The multiplication formula

```
1 core body
× 3 hooks           (different angles into the same body)
× 2 first frames    (what's seen at 0.0s)
× 2 CTAs            (end-card promise)
× 2 lengths         (e.g. 15s and 30s)
× 2 captions        (primary text)
= 48 possible assets
```

**48 is the *possible* count, not the count to launch.** Launching all 48 would split a growing brand's budget so thin that nothing gets enough data to judge. Use the **staged approach** below.

### Staged multiplication (what we actually launch)

| Stage | What varies | What stays constant | Assets | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1: Hook test** | 3 hooks × 2 first frames | Body, 1 length (the one most natural to the footage), 1 CTA, 1 caption | **6** | Hooks and first frames have the largest impact on attention. Test them first. |
| **Stage 2: Length test** | Top 2 hook+frame combos × 2 lengths | Body core, CTA, caption | **4** | Once the entry works, find the right depth of argument. |
| **Stage 3: Conversion polish** | Winner × 2 CTAs × 2 captions | Hook, first frame, length, body | **4** | CTA and caption mostly move CTR and conversion, not attention, so they're small levers tested last. |
| **Stage 4: Format expansion** | Winner → static, carousel, Story cut-down, AI UGC variant, regional language | The message | **4–8** | Take the proven message to new placements and audiences. |

**Total:** ~18–22 assets over 3–5 weeks, each one answering a question.

---

## What stays constant vs what changes

| Element | Constant or variable? | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Product | **Constant** | One product per ad. Never mix products inside a multiplication set. |
| Core angle / message | **Constant within a set** | If the message changes, it's a **new concept**, not a variation. |
| Body footage (mechanism, proof, taste) | **Constant** | Only trim for length. |
| Claims | **Constant** | Approved claims only; no variation "sneaks in" a new claim. |
| Hook (verbal + text) | **Variable** | Each hook must come from a different **hook category** or **tension lens**. |
| First frame | **Variable** | Must be visually different: appetite vs face vs pack vs text. |
| Length | **Variable** | Cut lengths from the same master edit. |
| CTA | **Variable** | Must differ in *type* (trial vs ritual vs label), not just wording. |
| Caption (primary text) | **Variable** | Must differ in *lead*: story-led vs proof-led. |
| Music / sound | Constant within a stage | Test separately only if an ad's hold rate is unusually low. |
| Creator | Constant within a set | A different creator = a new set (creator is a major variable). |

---

## Avoiding meaningless variations

**A variation is meaningful only if it tests a hypothesis you can write in one sentence.**

| ❌ Meaningless variation | ✅ Meaningful variation |
|---|---|
| "Try the cookies" vs "Try our cookies" | "Try a pack" (trial) vs "Keep the chai. Change the biscuit." (ritual) |
| Hook text in yellow vs white | Hook with a face vs hook with an appetite close-up |
| "5 PM happens" vs "5 PM happens." | "5 PM happens" (problem) vs "The four ingredients we didn't use" (curiosity) |
| The same hook with a 0.5s trim | 15s (taste-only) vs 30s (taste + label) |
| Changing the background music track | Adding ASMR crunch sound vs voiceover-only |

### The three tests for a variation
1. **Different category?** Does it change the *type* of hook, frame or CTA, not just the words?
2. **Would a viewer notice?** Put both side by side on a phone at scroll speed. If you can't tell them apart in one second, neither can the viewer.
3. **What would we learn?** If variant B wins, what would we do differently next time? If the answer is "nothing", don't make it.

> **Meta delivery note:** Meta's ad delivery increasingly groups near-identical creatives together and favours genuinely *different* creatives. Micro-variations don't just waste budget; they may not be delivered as separate tests at all. Diversity of **concept, format and first frame** matters more than volume.

## Worked example: Breakfast Cookies, "Keep the chai" shoot

**Core body (locked, ~20s):** swap sequence → back-of-pack → chai dunk → dad approval → pack end card.

**Stage 1 (6 assets):**

| # | Hook (category) | First frame |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Nobody in my house is giving up chai-biscuit." (cultural) | Slow-mo chai dunk macro |
| 2 | "Nobody in my house is giving up chai-biscuit." (cultural) | Creator's face mid-laugh with chai glass |
| 3 | "The four most important ingredients in this cookie are the ones we didn't use." (curiosity) | Four empty bowls labelled ✕ |
| 4 | Same as 3 | Pack flipped to the back |
| 5 | "My dad read the whole back of this pack." (humour/parent) | Dad squinting at the pack |
| 6 | Same as 5 | Text-only card on a warm background |

**Hypotheses being tested:**
- Which *angle* earns attention: ritual, curiosity or humour? (#1/2 vs #3/4 vs #5/6)
- Does food, face or text stop more people? (within each pair)

**Stage 2:** The winner (e.g. #5) cut at 15s and 30s.
**Stage 3:** The winner × CTA ("Try a pack" vs "Keep the chai. Change the biscuit.") × caption (Dad story-led vs ingredient-led).
**Stage 4:** The winning hook as a meme static ("My dad reading ingredients:"), as a carousel, as a Hindi voiceover, and as an AI UGC variant.

## Shoot-for-multiplication checklist
Every shoot must deliver the raw material for multiplication:
- [ ] 5+ spoken hook takes, from at least 3 hook categories
- [ ] 3+ visually distinct first-frame options (appetite, face, pack/label)
- [ ] A body recorded to work at 15s *and* 30s (modular: each block can be dropped)
- [ ] 3 CTA reads (trial, ritual, label)
- [ ] Clean audio and clean B-roll (no burnt-in text on raw files)
- [ ] Vertical 9:16 master with 4:5 and 1:1 safe framing
- [ ] Project file organised by block (Hook / Tension / Mechanism / Proof / Taste / CTA)

## Naming convention
```
PRODUCT_ANGLE_HOOKID_FRAME_LENGTH_CTA_VERSION
e.g.  BC_A34_HCT07_DUNK_15_RITUAL_v1
```
This makes every variant traceable in Ads Manager and in the learning log.
