# Part 11 — Creative Brief Template

Copy this template into a new file for each concept: `briefs/YYYY-MM-DD_PRODUCT_ANGLE.md`.
Fill **every** field. If a field can't be filled, the concept isn't ready.

The template works for marketers (strategy), designers (statics), editors (video), UGC creators (script and shots) and AI video tools (scene prompts).

---

## Filled example

```markdown
# CREATIVE BRIEF — "Dad Reads the Label"

Brief ID:        BR-2026-10-001
Product:         Millet & Oats Breakfast Cookies
Audience:        Adults 25–40 who buy snacks for their parents' house / live with
                 parents; enjoy family humour; chai drinkers
Funnel stage:    Prospecting
Consumer tension:T-01 — "Every snack I bring home gets inspected."
Current behaviour: Buys whatever biscuits are familiar; parents quietly judge
Old solution:    Regular packaged biscuits
Why it disappoints: Nobody in the house has actually read what's inside them
Insight:         Indian parents' label scrutiny is a lovable ritual, and passing it is a
                 badge of honour
Belief to shift: FROM "Snacks can't pass Dad's inspection" → TO "This one can, and
                 here's the label to prove it"
Angle:           A-33 Indian Parents Reading Labels
Route:           D Humour-led → B Label-led

Hook (primary):  "My dad reading the ingredients of anything I buy:"
Hook category:   Humour
Mechanism:       Millets + oats instead of maida · Jaggery instead of refined sugar ·
                 Cow butter, no palm oil
Proof:           Back-of-pack (real, current); Dad's genuine reaction
Objection:       "Isn't jaggery still sugar?"
Objection answer:Not addressed in 20s cut; addressed in caption P2
Emotional payoff:Warm family amusement; quiet pride
Key line:        "Hmm. Theek hai." (Dad's verdict)

Format:          UGC skit | 9:16 | 20s | Hinglish
Cast:            Real dad of creator, 58–68, reading glasses, sofa
Setting:         Living room, evening, chai on the side table
Pack first visible: 0s (in Dad's hands)
Brand first spoken: 14s

Scene 1 (0–3s):  Dad holds pack at arm's length, glasses pushed up, squinting.
                 OST: "My dad reading the ingredients of anything I buy:"
Scene 2 (3–10s): Dad reads slowly aloud: "Millets… oats… jaggery… cow butter…"
                 Finger moves down the label. Creator watches nervously off-frame.
Scene 3 (10–15s):Dad flips to front, flips back. Pause. "Hmm." Cut to enlarged label text
                 on screen. Creator VO: "Puŕ Ferme Millet & Oats Breakfast Cookies."
Scene 4 (15–20s):Dad takes a cookie, dunks in chai, eats. "Theek hai." Takes two more.
                 OST: "Dad-approved label." CTA: "Try the Breakfast Cookies."

CTA: "Pass the Dad test." / "Try a pack."   Button: Shop Now
Variants: H2 "The only snack my dad has ever approved of." (unexpected)
          H3 "When did you last read your biscuit packet? My dad reads every one." (question)
          F1 Dad squinting (face) · F2 Label macro with finger
          Lengths 15s / 20s · Captions: story-led / ingredient-led
Static: Format #4 Meme — "Me: buys snacks / My dad:" [squinting photo]

Hypothesis: Humour hook will beat category-average hook rate by making the label proof
            entertaining.
Success: Hook rate ≥ account median AND CPA ≤ target.
Kill: Hook rate lowest of batch after sufficient impressions.

Claims: millets, oats, jaggery, cow butter, no palm oil: all GREEN (verified)
Children: No | Testimonial: No (skit; Dad's reaction is genuine) | Disclosure: paid
partnership label
```
