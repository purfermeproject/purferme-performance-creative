# 20 Strongest Campaign Territories

A **territory** is bigger than an ad: a repeatable theme that can hold dozens of executions across products and formats. Ranked by expected performance potential × brand-building value × claim safety.

| # | Territory | Core idea | Products | Why it's strong | Example series / executions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Flip It** | Don't trust the front of any pack; read the back. Ours included. | All | Ownable brand behaviour; the proof is built in; no claims needed | #FlipIt weekly series; aisle flips; "our best-looking side" statics |
| 2 | **Keep the Chai, Change the Biscuit** | We don't ask you to give up rituals; we upgrade what's in them | BC | Culturally universal; zero preachiness; strong appetite visuals | Chai dunk test; verandah chai; office chai break |
| 3 | **The Dad (and Nani) Test** | The toughest label-readers in India are our parents | BC, CC, RS | Humour + proof; highly shareable; multi-generational | "Dad reads the label"; "Nani vs Mummy"; "Grandparent verdicts" |
| 4 | **Treat, Rethought** | It's a treat first. We just thought harder about what's in it. | CC, RS | Leads with desire; beats the "healthy = worse" bias | Macro breaks; "not an alternative"; late-night cookie confession |
| 5 | **Peanuts Doing Peanut Things** | PB never needed sugar | PB | Clear, rational, honest; strong for gym and breakfast | "Your PB vs PB"; "25g/100g"; post-gym toast |

## Where to start
**Test first:** 1 (Flip It), 2 (Keep the Chai), 3 (Dad Test), 4 (Treat, Rethought), 5 (Peanuts Doing Peanut Things).
These cover four products, all four routes, and the brand's most ownable idea, with mostly green claims.
