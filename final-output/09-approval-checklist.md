# Ad Approval Checklist

Run **every** ad (video, static, carousel, AI-generated) through this list before it goes live.
**Any ✗ in sections 1 or 2 = do not publish.** ✗ elsewhere = fix, or document why it's acceptable.

---

## 1. Claims and compliance (blocking)
- [ ] Every factual statement matches the **current** pack (ingredients, nutrition, "no" claims).
- [ ] No item from the [verification register](../13-verification-register.md) is used unless it's marked ✅.
- [ ] No 🔴 RED words or claims: healthy, guilt-free, sugar-free, superfood, energy, growth, immunity, digestion, weight, diabetes, disease, "natural" (unless cleared), fear words (toxic, chemicals, poison, junk).
- [ ] Every 🟡 YELLOW claim has documented substantiation on file.
- [ ] Sugar wording is correct for the product (jaggery products: "no refined sugar", never "no added sugar"/"sugar-free"; PB: "no added sugar", never "sugar-free").
- [ ] No competitor is named, shown or recognisable. Comparisons are against a generic category only.
- [ ] Testimonials and reviews are real, consented, unedited in meaning, and contain no health claims.
- [ ] No invented numbers, ratings, customer counts, experts or certifications.
- [ ] Sunrise Bowl: written legal clearance on file. No infants shown. No development, digestion or nutrition claims.
- [ ] Children in the ad: parental consent, and Meta and ASCI rules followed. No pester-power, no encouraging excessive consumption.
- [ ] Paid/gifted creators: disclosure is clear and upfront (ASCI).
- [ ] AI-generated content: labelled where required. No AI avatar presents a fake personal experience. The pack shown is the real pack.
- [ ] Targeting and copy don't imply personal health attributes ("Are you diabetic?").

## 2. Truthfulness (blocking)
- [ ] The hook is **fully delivered** by the body (no clickbait).
- [ ] Every "real" moment (reaction, empty tiffin, dad's approval, kid's second cookie) actually happened.
- [ ] The food shown is the real product, not styled to look better than it is (no fake fillings, no enhanced texture).
- [ ] The offer/price/CTA promise matches the landing page exactly.

## 3. Strategy: Stop / Care / Believe / Act
- [ ] **Stop:** The first 1.5 seconds has movement and one clear focal point (appetite, face, pack or bold text).
- [ ] **Stop:** The hook is readable **sound-off** and speaks to a *specific* person or moment.
- [ ] **Care:** The tension is true at its real size, with no exaggeration, fear or shame.
- [ ] **Believe:** At least one visible proof is present (back-of-pack, nutrition panel, taste reaction, real review).
- [ ] **Believe:** The mechanism is a real ingredient swap, not an invented benefit.
- [ ] **Act:** The CTA is specific, low-risk and matched to the funnel stage.
- [ ] There's one product per ad and one main message.

## 4. Brand
- [ ] Taste is shown, not just claimed.
- [ ] Tone: warm, intelligent, slightly playful. Not preachy, not clinical, not hype.
- [ ] Indian setting, props and people. Diverse household roles (not only mothers).
- [ ] It doesn't disparage traditional Indian foods, rituals or anyone's current choices.
- [ ] It uses "made differently / rethought / ingredients you can read", not "healthy".
- [ ] It passes the "would we be proud if a journalist or regulator shared this?" test.

## 5. Craft
- [ ] Pack visible early (0–3s) unless it's a deliberate blind/reveal format.
- [ ] Pack reveal held ≥ 1.5s, logo readable on a phone, no glare, **current** design.
- [ ] Back-of-pack shown with enlarged, legible text overlay (2–3s).
- [ ] Captions and on-screen text inside the 9:16 safe zone (clear of the top ~14% and bottom ~20%).
- [ ] Text is legible at phone size (contrast, size, ≤ 7 words per card).
- [ ] Audio: clean VO, crunch/ASMR moments audible, music doesn't bury speech.
- [ ] End card ≤ 2s.
- [ ] Exported in all required ratios (9:16, 4:5, 1:1).
- [ ] Primary text: the first 125 characters work as a second hook.

## 6. Testing readiness
- [ ] Named using the convention `PRODUCT_ANGLE_HOOKID_FRAME_LENGTH_CTA_VERSION`.
- [ ] Linked to a brief with a written hypothesis and success/kill criteria.
- [ ] The variable being tested is clear, and it's the **only** variable that differs from its sibling.
- [ ] Landing page checked (loads fast, matches the message, back of pack visible).
- [ ] Logged in the learning log.
