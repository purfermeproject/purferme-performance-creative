# 30-Day Creative Testing Roadmap

**Assumptions:** a lean team (1 marketer/media buyer, 1 designer/editor, freelance UGC creators), a growing Meta budget, and the testing structure from [Part 9](../08-creative-testing.md). Adjust the batch sizes to the budget using the table in Part 9.

---

## Pre-launch: Days −7 to 0 (preparation week)

| Task | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Verify BC-01–08, PB-01–03, RS-01–02, B-01, B-06–09 | Marketer + founders | Updated [verification register](../13-verification-register.md) |
| Start legal/regulatory review for Sunrise Bowl (SB-01–03) and wording (B-10) | Founders + advisor | Written opinion (may take longer than 30 days) |
| Build account baselines (last 30–60 days, per product) | Media buyer | Baseline sheet: hook rate, hold, CTR, ATC rate, CPA median + control |
| Set target CPA per product from margin and repeat rate | Founders + media buyer | Target CPA table |
| Landing page check: speed, message match, back-of-pack visible, price clarity | Marketer | Fix list |
| Write briefs for the first 10 ads | Marketer | 5 concept briefs ([template](../10-creative-brief-template.md)) |
| Book creators and shoots | Marketer | 3 shoots scheduled |
| Set up testing and scaling campaigns, naming convention and learning log | Media buyer | Account structure |

---

## Week 1 (Days 1–7): Shoot, edit and launch batch 1

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Shoots: Breakfast Cookies (family), PB (gym creator), Spread (parent + child). Capture **all multiplication shots**. |
| 3–5 | Edit the first 10 ads ([list](05-first-10-ads.md)). Run every ad through the [approval checklist](09-approval-checklist.md). |
| 5–6 | Launch **batch 1**: 5 concepts, 10 ads (1 video + 1 static per concept), one ad set per concept, equal budgets. |
| 7 | Daily check only (spend pacing, broken ads, comments). **No edits.** |

**Hypotheses logged before launch:**
- H1: Breakfast Cookies will have the lowest CPA (broadest occasion, clearest swap).
- H2: The humour route (Dad Test) will have the highest hook rate.
- H3: The PB gym angle will have the highest CTR (a rational, specific audience).
- H4: Statics will get lower CPMs, and video lower CPAs.

---

## Week 2 (Days 8–14): Read attention signals, multiply hooks

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 8 | **Monday review.** Read hook rate, hold rate and CTR vs siblings and baseline. **Early kill** any ad whose hook rate is far below all siblings. Purchase data is not yet conclusive; don't judge CPA. |
| 9 | Create **hook variants** (Part 8, stage 1) for the **two strongest concepts by attention**: 3 new hooks × 2 first frames each, from existing footage. |
| 10–11 | Edit and approve. |
| 12 | Launch **batch 2a**: hook variants (as new ads in the concept's ad set, or a new ad set if the original is still learning). |
| 12–14 | If Chocolate Cookies are verified: brief and shoot the "Treat, Rethought" concept. |

---

## Week 3 (Days 15–21): First conversion decisions, and a new concepts batch

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 15 | **Monday review.** Batch 1 has ~10–14 days of data. Apply the [decision tree](../09-winner-iteration.md): **keep / kill / modify / expand / reshoot** for each ad. Identify the **provisional winner(s)** vs target CPA. |
| 15 | Diagnose failures by metric-chain position: hook, body, page, offer or audience ([diagnosis table](../08-creative-testing.md#5-diagnosis-whats-actually-broken)). |
| 16 | Graduate any ad beating target CPA to the **scaling campaign** (keep the post ID). |
| 16–18 | Produce **batch 2b: new concepts** (3 concepts): Chocolate Cookies "Treat, Rethought" *(if verified)*, Breakfast Cookies "5 PM Plan", Brand "Honest Cookie". |
| 19 | Launch batch 2b. |
| 20–21 | **Stage 2 multiplication** on the week-2 hook winners: length variants (15s vs 30s). |

---

## Week 4 (Days 22–30): Expand winners, polish, plan month 2

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 22 | **Monday review.** Batch 2a (hooks) and length tests have readable data. Confirm winners. |
| 23–25 | **Expand** the strongest message (Scenario 7): take the winning territory to (a) a second product, (b) a new format (meme/carousel), (c) a Hindi or regional language version. |
| 25 | **Stage 3 multiplication** on the confirmed winner: 2 CTAs × 2 captions. |
| 26 | Check **fatigue** on scaling ads (frequency, CTR trend). Queue entry-point refreshes. |
| 27–28 | **Month-1 learning review:** which product, route, territory, hook category and format won? Update the [top hooks](04-top-50-hooks.md) and [territories](03-campaign-territories.md) with real results. |
| 29–30 | Plan **month 2**: reshoot winners with new creators, launch Chocolate Cookies fully, Sunrise Bowl (if legally cleared), AI UGC for low-cost angle tests, retargeting creatives (objection handling, reviews, oil-on-top education). |

---

## End-of-month deliverables
- [ ] Learning log with every test, hypothesis and result
- [ ] 2–4 proven ads in the scaling campaign
- [ ] Updated baselines per product
- [ ] A ranked list: product × route × territory × format
- [ ] Month-2 brief pipeline (6–10 briefs)
- [ ] Verification register updated (ideally Chocolate Cookies fully verified)

## What *not* to do this month
- Don't launch more concepts than the budget can read (2–3× target CPA per concept).
- Don't edit live ads; duplicate and relaunch instead.
- Don't judge CPA before ~7 days and sufficient spend.
- Don't run Sunrise Bowl ads before legal clearance.
- Don't scale a winner by editing its budget up sharply every day; increase gradually, or duplicate into the scaling campaign.
