# Reusable AI Creative Prompt

The full prompt (inputs, hard rules, framework and 11-part output format), plus a short hook-brainstorm version and an AI video scene-prompt pattern, is in **[`11-ai-prompt-template.md`](../11-ai-prompt-template.md)**.

## How to use it (3 steps)

1. **Copy** the full prompt from `11-ai-prompt-template.md`.
2. **Fill the inputs:** product, *verified facts only* (paste from the product file), angle, audience, platform, duration, aspect ratio, objective, format, language.
3. **Attach context** (strongly recommended): the product file from `products/` and `12-claims-guardrails.md`.

## What you get back
1. Strategy summary (tension, insight, belief shift, route, Stop/Care/Believe/Act)
2. 10 hook options across 5+ categories (top 3 starred)
3. A timed script table
4. A shot list (usable as AI video prompts)
5. On-screen text
6. Voiceover
7. 3 CTA options
8. Edit notes
9. A static adaptation
10. 3 variations, each testing a different hypothesis
11. A compliance check (every claim colour-coded)

## After generation
- Replace every `[REQUIRES VERIFICATION]` with a verified fact, or cut the line.
- Run the output through the [approval checklist](09-approval-checklist.md).
- Never publish an AI-rendered pack; composite the real pack or use real footage.
