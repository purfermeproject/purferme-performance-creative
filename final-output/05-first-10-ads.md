# The First 10 Ads to Produce

**Selection logic:**
- Weighted toward products whose facts are **mostly verified** (Breakfast Cookies, Unsweetened PB).
- Covers **all four routes** and **multiple formats**, so the first tests teach us what *type* of creative works, not just which ad.
- **5 distinct concepts × 2 executions** (one video + one static each), so every concept is tested in two formats.
- **Sunrise Bowl is deliberately excluded** until legal clearance (SB-01–03). **Chocolate Cookies** enter in batch 2, once CC-01–05 are verified; if verified before launch, swap Ad 9/10 for them.

## Questions batch 1 answers
1. **Which product acquires most efficiently?** (BC vs PB vs RS)
2. **Which route works best for Breakfast Cookies?** Routine (Ad 1) vs humour (Ad 3) vs label (Ad 5)
3. **Video vs static?** Each concept has both
4. **Does our signature "read the back" idea perform, or just build brand?** (Ads 5, 6)

## Production plan
- **One Breakfast Cookie shoot day** covers Ads 1, 3 and 5 (same kitchen/living room, one creator family including a dad/grandparent), plus all the multiplication shots ([Part 8 checklist](../07-creative-multiplication.md#shoot-for-multiplication-checklist)).
- **One PB shoot** (gym creator, home kitchen) covers Ad 7.
- **One Spread shoot** (parent creator + child) covers Ad 9.
- **Statics** (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) come from the shoot stills plus a product photography session.
- **Before shooting:** complete verification items BC-01–08, PB-01–03, RS-01–02 and B-01.
